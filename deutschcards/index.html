Deutsch Cards

Learn German vocabulary visually, with picture cards, your own collections and a simple review mode.

Get it on Google Play

Free for Android · works offline

What it does

  • Picture cardsEvery word on a card with a picture, so it's easier to remember.
  • CollectionsGroup words the way you study them, by topic or by lesson.
  • Review modeGo through your cards again to keep the words fresh.
  • Offline firstEverything works without internet. Back up to your own Google Drive if you like.
